The ACS 2001 dataset has 644,427 prime-age individuals out of the total sample of 1,192,206 individuals. Of these, 73,101 individuals moved residences. Of the 73,101 individuals who moved, 80.6\% migrated within their states. We observe a total of 14,215 between-state movers out of the 644,427 prime-age individuals in the 2001 ACS.
